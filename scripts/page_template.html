Whole fields, drawn from the posterior

A GPO is a Gaussian process over the solution operator of a PDE. Its posterior returns joint draws of the output field: four prior draws of Navier–Stokes vorticity become exact posterior draws by one correction, \(f + k(\cdot, X)K^{-1}(y - f(X) - \varepsilon)\).

Abstract

The growing demand for accurate, efficient, and scalable solutions in computational mechanics highlights the need for advanced operator learning algorithms that can efficiently handle large datasets while providing reliable uncertainty quantification. This paper introduces a novel Gaussian Process (GP) based neural operator for solving parametric differential equations. The approach proposed leverages the expressive capability of deterministic neural operators and the uncertainty awareness of conventional GP. In particular, we propose a “neural operator-embedded kernel” wherein the GP kernel is formulated in the latent space learned using a neural operator. Further, we exploit a stochastic dual descent (SDD) algorithm for simultaneously training the neural operator parameters and the GP hyperparameters. Our approach addresses the (a) resolution dependence and (b) cubic complexity of traditional GP models, allowing for input-resolution independence and scalability in high-dimensional and non-linear parametric systems, such as those encountered in computational mechanics. We apply our method to a range of non-linear parametric partial differential equations (PDEs) and demonstrate its superiority in both computational efficiency and accuracy compared to standard GP models and wavelet neural operators. Our experimental results highlight the efficacy of this framework in solving complex PDEs while maintaining robustness in uncertainty estimation, positioning it as a scalable and reliable operator-learning algorithm for computational mechanics.

Matheron's rule

A posterior draw is a prior draw plus a correction computed from its residuals at the data.

\[ f \mid y \;=\; \underbrace{f}_{\text{prior draw}} \;+\; \underbrace{k(\cdot, X)\,K^{-1}\big(y - f(X) - \varepsilon\big)}_{\text{correction}}, \qquad K = k(X, X) + \sigma^2 I \]

One draw, step by step. A prior draw \(f\) disagrees with the data by the residuals \(r = y - f(X) - \varepsilon\), where \(\varepsilon\) is a draw of the observation noise. The kernel spreads the residuals into a smooth correction \(k(\cdot, X)K^{-1}r\) that matches them at the data and fades away from it. The sum follows the data where there is data and keeps the prior's variability elsewhere. Repeating with fresh \((f, \varepsilon)\) gives independent draws that fill the exact posterior.

For operators

Each training example is a pair of functions, the input \(a\) and its output field \(u\). The same rule conditions a prior over operators on all training pairs at once, and every draw is a whole output field.

Method

A zero-mean Gaussian process whose kernel is built from a neural operator.

\[ k\big((a,x),(a',x')\big) \;=\; \phi(a,x)^\top \Lambda\, \phi(a',x') \;+\; \big[\, s_a^2\, k_a\big(\psi(a), \psi(a')\big) + s_e^2\, \mathbb{1}[a = a'] \,\big]\, k_x(x, x') \]
Kernel

Operator-embedded

\(\phi\) are the pointwise features of a wavelet neural operator and \(\psi\) an embedding of them. The first term is a Bayesian projection layer; the second correlates inputs through the operator's latent space and output points through \(k_x\).

Exact

No approximation

\(K = K_a \otimes K_x + \Psi\Psi^\top + \sigma^2 I\) is handled with two eigendecompositions and a rank-\(c\) Woodbury correction: likelihood, gradients, posterior and draws without forming the \(Nd \times Nd\) matrix.

Scalable

Stochastic dual descent

For large \(N\), stochastic dual descent solves the same system with operator features streamed, in \(\mathcal{O}(Nd)\) memory.

Where the model is unsure

On the notched domain and on wave advection the error concentrates near boundaries and discontinuities, and the posterior learns an amplitude that widens the interval there. On Navier–Stokes, Burgers and Darcy the error is statistically homogeneous over the field; the uncertainty then lies in its size and spatial correlation, which the sampled errors in the gallery show.

Predicted σ against actual error, one point per test field: σ ranks the hard cases. On Navier–Stokes and Darcy the error varies little between test fields and σ is nearly constant.

Applications

Sensor fusion

A few measurements of the unknown output correct the whole predicted field exactly, with no retraining. Sensors go where the posterior variance is largest; every draw is updated by Matheron's rule.

Error and 95% coverage against the number of sensors, 50 test fields. Baseline: the operator corrected by kriging its residual at the sensors. On Navier–Stokes, 128 uncertainty-guided sensors reduce the error from 0.30% to 0.12%, against 0.15% for random sensors and for residual kriging.

Quantities computed from the field

Intervals for an energy, a maximum or a gradient need joint draws. Draws with the right pointwise variance but independent grid points give miscalibrated intervals.

Coverage of 90% intervals over the test set. Blue: joint Matheron draws. Orange: same pointwise mean and variance, independent across grid points.

New simulations without retraining

New fields enter through the posterior. Stochastic dual descent conditions on tens of thousands of fields where the exact solve runs out of memory.

Any input resolution

Trained on one discretisation, the GPO takes input functions sampled on coarser or finer grids, zero-shot, and predicts the output on its training grid.

What is new in this release

  • KernelThe operator-embedded kernel is extended with an operator-feature term, a spatial factor, a per-input term and, where the held-out evidence supports it, an amplitude that varies with the input and the position. The prior stays zero-mean.
  • InferenceExact and numerically stable likelihood, posterior and draws; stochastic dual descent for large \(N\).
  • SamplingMatheron's rule with prior draws from the learned kernel and sampled observation noise gives exact joint posterior draws, verified against a dense Gaussian process.
  • EvaluationStandard splits, three seeds, held-out evidence for the kernel hyperparameters and an input-resolution study. The code accompanying the journal article remains in the repository history.
